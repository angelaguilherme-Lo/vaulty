# Vaulty support

[Open a support request](https://github.com/angelaguilherme-Lo/vaulty/issues). Do not include credentials, personal documents, or private notes in public issues.

Vaulty requires an iPhone passcode and uses Face ID, Touch ID, or device authentication to unlock its local workspace. There is no cloud account or remote recovery service.

Export an encrypted backup from Settings and keep it outside this device. Use a separate passphrase with at least 12 characters. To restore, unlock Vaulty on the destination device, open Settings, choose the backup file, and enter its passphrase. Restoring replaces existing entries. Forgotten backup passphrases cannot be recovered.

Removing your device passcode may delete the workspace encryption key. If the key is unavailable, reinstall Vaulty and restore a backup. Without a usable backup, the developer cannot recover the workspace.

Files up to 5 MB each are supported. The encoded workspace limit is 20 MB. Optional local alerts schedule the next 60 future reminders and show a generic prompt without reminder details. Open Vaulty to refresh the schedule and check iOS notification/Focus settings if an alert does not appear.
