# Vaulty privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026. Developer: Angela Guilherme.

Vaulty is a local-only iPhone workspace for notes, reminders, documents, and credentials. Workspace entries are encrypted on the device. Its encryption key is protected by iOS Keychain and device authentication.

Vaulty does not operate a cloud account service and does not transmit workspace entries to the developer. It includes no analytics, advertising, tracking, or external AI service. Its offline guide contains bundled tips.

Notification permission is optional. Local reminder notifications contain a generic prompt to open the app and do not include entry titles. Permissions can be changed in iPhone Settings.

Imported files are copied into the encrypted workspace. When you export a document, the selected file is shared with the destination you choose through iOS. Exported workspace backups are encrypted with a separate passphrase you choose. You control where you save them. iOS clipboard entries for copied passwords are local to the device and expire after one minute.

You can delete individual entries inside Vaulty. There is no trash or developer-side copy. Local workspace files are excluded from automatic device backups. Keep your own encrypted backup outside the device. Removing your device passcode, losing the device, or deleting the app can make local data unrecoverable. Vaulty cannot recover lost keys or forgotten backup passphrases. Exported copies remain with the destinations you choose until you delete them there.

The browser preview keeps workspace entries in memory and clears them when locked or reloaded. Theme preference may be stored locally by your browser.

For support or privacy questions, use the [Vaulty support tracker](https://github.com/angelaguilherme-Lo/vaulty/issues). This is a public tracker: do not include passwords, personal notes, documents, or other sensitive information in a request.
